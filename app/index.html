How the check works
  • A payment counts toward an invoice only when it names that invoice and the same vendor. IDs are compared without spaces or capitals.
  • Difference = payments − amount billed, in whole cents. Above zero is Overpaid; zero is Paid in full; below zero is a Remaining balance.
  • The same payment ID imported twice counts once. The same ID with different details is held for review.
  • Two invoice records with the same vendor and invoice number are a Possible duplicate bill.
  • Unclear payments, refunds and credits hold only the invoices they could affect, with a recommended next step.
  • Flags are possible problems for a reviewer, not proof of fraud. Saved reviews stay in this browser.
